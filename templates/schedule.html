Parallel Tracks Notice 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM IST

Concurrent Multi-Track Conference Architecture

Please note that University Student Presentations commence at 9:45 AM in parallel with the Inaugural Ceremony commencing at 10:00 AM in the main auditorium. These parallel tracks occur simultaneously across designated campus locations.

Track 1: Auditorium Sessions Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Auditorium (Inauguration, Keynotes, Valedictory)
Track 2: SNPSU Student Presentations 2nd, 3rd & 4th Floors (42 Sections) | 5th & 6th Floors (Allied Branches)
Track 3: Outside Participants 6th Floor Dedicated Classrooms (Break: 11:30–11:45 AM | Sessions: 12:00–1:00 PM)
SNPSU Students Track 9:45 AM – 11:45 AM

Student Project & Paper Presentations

  • Total Duration: 2 Hours (10 minutes allocation per batch)
  • Scope: 42 Sections distributed across classrooms
  • Location: Existing classrooms from 2nd Floor to 4th Floor
  • Allied Branches: Classrooms located on 5th & 6th Floors
  • Room Allocation: Individual section-to-room assignments will be announced officially.
Outside Delegates Track 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM

Outside Participant Technical Sessions

  • Dedicated Refreshment Break: 11:30 AM – 11:45 AM (for outside participants)
  • Oral Presentations: 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM
  • Venue: 6th Floor Dedicated Conference Rooms
  • Room Allocation: 6th floor room allocations will be announced prior to the conference.
Auditorium Ceremonies Confirmed Segments

Ceremonial Protocol & Timings

Inaugural Protocol (Starts 10:00 AM): Guest Speaker address (~30 mins) • Key dignitaries (~10 mins each) • Maximum total duration: ~1 hr 15 mins
Lunch Intermission: 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM (University Dining Hall & Banquet Facility)
Valedictory Session (Starts 3:00 PM): Guest Speaker (15 mins) • Prize Distribution (30 mins) • Report Reading (10 mins) • Conclusion at 4:00 PM
