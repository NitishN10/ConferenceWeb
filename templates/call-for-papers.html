Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished research papers related to the conference themes and tracks. Submissions must adhere to the formatting and submission guidelines outlined below.

Paper Format & Specifications

  • Paper Format: Standard double-column conference format. A downloadable Word template is provided below.
  • Page Limit: Full research papers should strictly adhere to the prescribed conference length (typically 6 to 8 pages including figures, tables, and references).
  • Accepted File Formats: Manuscripts must be submitted in PDF, DOC, or DOCX format. Maximum file size: 16 MB.
  • Originality: All papers must represent original research not published or under review elsewhere. Plagiarism or duplicate submission is strictly prohibited.

Review Process

All submitted manuscripts will undergo a double-blind peer review process conducted by reviewers and program committee members. Papers will be evaluated on technical soundness, relevance to conference tracks, originality, research methodology, and presentation clarity.

Publication Information

All accepted, registered, and presented papers will be included in the official conference proceedings. Specific publication partner and indexation details will be announced.
