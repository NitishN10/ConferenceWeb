Conference Overview

Welcome to {{ CONF_BRAND }}

The {{ CONF_NAME }} is hosted and organized by Sapthagiri NPS University (SNPSU), Bengaluru on {{ CONF_DATE }}. The conference serves as a premier international academic and research forum for scholars, faculty members, researchers, and technical specialists to present and deliberate on foundational and applied innovations in large-scale data engineering.

With the exponential expansion of digital datasets across healthcare, scientific computing, enterprise platforms, and IoT networks, developing reliable, scalable, and secure data tools is paramount. {{ CONF_BRAND }} brings together original contributions spanning Big Data Architectures, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science & Big Data, Computer Vision, Cloud & Distributed Systems, and Intelligent Solutions.

Rigorous Peer Review: High-integrity double-blind review process by an international program committee.
Conference Proceedings: All accepted, registered, and presented papers published in official conference proceedings.
Recognition & Awards: Best Research Paper and Outstanding Student Innovation awards presented during the Valedictory session.
