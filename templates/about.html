Introduction

Saptha DataZen 2.6

The National Conference in Emerging Trends of Big Data Engineering 2.6 (Saptha DataZen 2.6) is an academic and research conference organized and hosted by Sapthagiri NPS University, Bangalore.

The conference is focused on recent developments, tools, frameworks, and methodologies across Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science & Big Data, Computer Vision, Generative AI, Cloud and Distributed Technologies, IoT & Edge Computing, and Cybersecurity.

Purpose of the Conference

The primary purpose of the conference is to bring together researchers, academicians, students, data scientists, software engineers, and industry professionals from around the country to present and deliberate on original research contributions, state-of-the-art architectures, and innovative data-driven solutions.

Research Scope & Thematic Tracks

Proposed Conference Topics

The {{ CONF_NAME }} ({{ CONF_BRAND }}) welcomes original research papers, architectural prototypes, empirical studies, and innovative student team project submissions across the following 13 multidisciplinary research domains:

Topic 01

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Deep learning architectures, neural networks, reinforcement learning, predictive algorithms, explainable AI, and autonomous decision systems.

Topic 02

Data Science, Big Data and Data Analytics

Large-scale data pipelines, statistical modeling, data lakes, business intelligence, streaming analytics, and knowledge graph engineering.

Topic 03

Computer Vision and Image Processing

Object detection, medical imaging analytics, pattern recognition, image segmentation, video surveillance, and biometric visual processing.

Topic 04

Natural Language Processing and Generative AI

Large Language Models (LLMs), semantic analysis, machine translation, generative multi-modal agents, sentiment analysis, and speech processing.

Topic 05

Cybersecurity, Privacy and Digital Forensics

Threat modeling, intrusion detection systems, differential privacy, forensic data recovery, cryptography, and secure software architectures.

Topic 06

Internet of Things and Edge Computing

Smart sensory networks, edge intelligence, fog computing topologies, stream processing at scale, and low-latency cyber-physical systems.

Topic 07

Cloud Computing and Distributed Systems

Serverless computing, multi-cloud orchestration, microservices, containerization, distributed consensus, and fault-tolerant cloud clusters.

Topic 08

Blockchain and Emerging Computing Technologies

Distributed ledgers, smart contracts, decentralized applications (dApps), quantum computing paradigms, and decentralized identity governance.

Topic 09

Computer Networks and Communication Systems

5G/6G wireless networks, software-defined networking (SDN), network telemetry, latency optimization, and next-generation protocols.

Topic 10

Software Engineering, Testing and Agile Development

DevOps/MLOps automation, automated regression verification, software architecture metrics, code smells detection, and continuous delivery.

Topic 11

Human–Computer Interaction and User Experience

Accessibility engineering, multimodal interaction design, cognitive load modeling, user telemetry, and intuitive assistive user interfaces.

Topic 12

Extended Reality, Virtual Reality and Spatial Computing

Immersive 3D environments, spatial mapping, augmented reality applications, digital twins, haptic feedback systems, and holographic visualization.

Topic 13

Robotics, Automation and Intelligent Systems

Autonomous mobile robots, industrial automation, sensory perception, drone navigation algorithms, swarm intelligence, and robotic process automation.

Direction

Vision, Objectives & Expected Outcomes

Vision

To create an academic and research forum that promotes excellence in big data analytics, artificial intelligence, and data science, fostering innovations that address complex challenges in science, industry, and society.

Objectives

  • Provide a platform for presentation of peer-reviewed research papers.
  • Discuss practical and theoretical advances in big data architectures and AI.
  • Foster academic-industry knowledge exchange and collaborative research.
  • Highlight emerging trends in cloud analytics, distributed systems, and security.

Expected Outcomes

Publication of high-quality peer-reviewed research, identification of future research directions in big data tools and techniques, and strengthened networking between academic institutions and industry leaders.
